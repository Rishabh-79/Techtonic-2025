About Us

The Cybernauts Student Association of the Department of Computer Science is committed to inspiring students to delve deeper into the dynamic world of computer science, foster innovation, develop impactful technological solutions, and cultivate a genuine passion for the field. As an initiative to complement and enrich the academic curriculum, the association is organizing an Inter-Collegiate Technical Competition titled "Techtonic – The Epicenter of Innovation", exclusively for undergraduate Computer Science students from Arts and Science colleges in and around Chennai.

The event is scheduled to be held on 25th September 2026 at Anderson Hall, Madras Christian College.

The term "Techtonic" embodies the profound and transformative influence of technology on the world, evoking the idea of a seismic shift that drives significant advancements across the technological landscape. The event seeks to explore, celebrate, and showcase groundbreaking innovations that are redefining the future of technology.

Featuring six diverse technical events, Techtonic provides an engaging and competitive platform for students to demonstrate their technical expertise, creativity, and innovative thinking. It also offers an excellent opportunity to exhibit, promote, and advertise your products, while connecting with a vibrant community of aspiring technologists and innovators.
